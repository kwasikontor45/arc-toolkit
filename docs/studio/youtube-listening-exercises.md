# YouTube listening exercises

These are listening references for original beat-making practice. The Studio panel opens each source in your browser. No audio is downloaded, copied into LMMS, or sent to a service.

## 1. Libianca — “People”

[Open the video](https://youtu.be/hqXDTkFzCfQ)

Listen for the vocal phrasing, the space between lines, and how the groove supports a reflective mood. In a new LMMS project, make an original 8-bar pattern with a restrained first half and a fuller second half. Leave room in the arrangement; do not recreate the recording, melody, or lyrics.

## 2. Becky G and Peso Pluma — “CHANEL”

[Open the official video](https://youtu.be/b2wQtu9YnWk)

Listen for the pulse, repeated rhythmic hooks, and how the arrangement changes between sections. Build a separate original 8-bar idea in LMMS: keep the first pass sparse, then change one drum or bass element for the second pass. Make your own notes and melody.

## A simple session

1. Open LMMS from **Khaos Lab → Studio** and choose **File → New**.
2. Listen to a short section in the browser. Tap the pulse and write down a rough tempo and the instruments or rhythm roles you hear.
3. Create an original kick, snare, and hat pattern. Save the project under a new name in your own work folder.
4. Make one quieter section and one fuller section. Keep the master meter out of the red.
5. Export a short WAV only when you want to listen outside LMMS. Keep the editable `.mmp` project.

Use an artist's official instrumental, stems, or a separately licensed file if you want to edit the source audio. A public video link by itself is not permission to extract or reuse its audio.
